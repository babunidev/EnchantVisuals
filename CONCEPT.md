# EnchantVisuals — concept

**Author:** Babuni.dev

A native cosmetic mod for the 32-bit WoW 1.12.1 (5875) client that allows permanent weapon enchant glows to coexist with Shaman imbue visuals.

## Source-observed constraints

This is not a Lua addon. Narrow binary compatibility and conservative installer safeguards are central; third-party MinHook and WoW assets retain their own credits.

This is not an invented account of early discussion.
